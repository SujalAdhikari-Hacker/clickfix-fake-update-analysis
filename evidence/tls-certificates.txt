WHY THIS MATTERS
----------------
The SAN list is the tell. A site that only needed to serve a web page would ask
for dateseni.su and www.dateseni.su. This certificate also covers:

    mail.dateseni.su          -> SMTP/IMAP
    owa.dateseni.su           -> Outlook Web Access
    autodiscover.dateseni.su  -> Outlook client auto-config

That is the standard hostname set for a *mail server impersonating Exchange*.
Translation: the same domain was built to run mass email campaigns and a fake
Outlook Web Access login page, not just to host one redirect.

Also note the cert lifetime. The GlobalSign cert is a 200-day DV cert issued in
March; the Let's Encrypt cert was re-issued in June after (presumably) the
first one became inconvenient. Short-lived free DV certs mean the operators can
rotate identity cheaply the moment abuse reports land.

The domain is younger than the certificates suggest at first glance — a
2026-03 issuance means the *infrastructure* is at most ~8 months old as of
this analysis, and the June re-issue suggests active, ongoing operation.
